- **Snowy owl NPC — shipped 9/20, made visible + given a little life 9/20, fully reworked toward realism 9/21**: a snowy owl perched on a snow-capped low branch of a dedicated tall tree in a new clearing right past the door. (em dash removed per direct feedback). Follow-up ("we cant really see the snow owl it blends into the snowy tree too much"): the perch branch was buried in the canopy's own droopy lower foliage — moved higher up the trunk and extended out much further so the owl now sits clearly against open sky. Follow-up ("branch is like stabbing owl"): the owl was raised well above the branch tip and re-centered so the branch's curve stops at its feet instead of running through its body; branch lowered slightly and the owl made bigger per direct follow-up. Full realism rework **9/21** ("just being white doesnt work... oak owl"): real plumage texture (barred/mottled feather strokes), a cream-to-buff body gradient, layered folded wings, visible legs/talons, facial-disc detail, and an eye highlight. The idle shuffle was reworked from a simple reposition into a decaying shake — a short burst roughly every 7s that snaps back to rest rather than sliding. The owl's tree was also moved further right, and its speech bubble lowered a bit more.
- **Content shifted right for breathing room — 9/20**: the front treeline, the slick-platform climb, and the icicle pocket all moved further from the door (a single shared offset), opening up the new clearing for the owl's tree instead of trees/platforms crowding in immediately.
- **Two low-stakes practice platforms in the breathing room — 9/20**: a small taste of the slick-platform feel (drift included) placed between the owl's tree and the real climb, deliberately outside the spike hazard zone so there's no fall risk yet — lets the player feel "this is slippery" before the real climb raises the stakes.
- **A little tree variety — 9/20**: winter pines now get a small bare twig or two poking out of the canopy (stable per tree, subtle), and the owl's own tree got two extra decorative branch stubs so it reads as its own distinct set-piece rather than a scaled-up copy of a normal pine.
- Slick icy platforms with real slip (momentum-carry + platform drift), now 12 platforms total (10 real + 2 low-stakes practice ones in the breathing room), drifting, zigzagging in height/width, descending back toward ground before a breathing-room stretch
- Spiky ice ground hazards under the whole platform climb — miss your footing and you reset to the base of the climb; gaps carved out around trees so they still work as a bail-out foothold; reworked visually into real jagged, clustered, non-uniform spikes with per-spike shine/shadow variation
- Rainbow dripping icicles in a rock-ledge pocket, reworked to match a piece of Sam's own reference art (organic tendril shapes, bold outlines, blocked color, contrasting bulb-tip color), with a slower/more realistic build-up-then-drip animation. Later reworked away from that bold-outline "wine bottle" look toward the game's own established icicle language (faceted, jaggedly-tapering shards, always connected at the top via a real ice fringe). Then, after still reading as flat "pasted-on triangles" (**9/20**): the bodies/fringe/webs were routed through real smooth curves instead of straight polygon edges, each icicle's width now builds from a narrow neck instead of snapping to full width immediately, and each one got real per-icicle light/shadow shading (like a faceted 3D sliver, not a flat color wash) plus a soft icy rim for edge definition. Follow-up fix **9/20** ("that concave shape doesnt make sense here"): the rock mass the icicles hang from could occasionally roll a big smooth concave "bite" out of its underside instead of small rock-like facets — tightened up so it stays genuinely jagged.
- Translucent "watery" ice under the rainbow icicle pocket — an ongoing ambient current that gently slip-slides you around while standing on it (not a one-shot launch), colors melding together from the icicles above; built as a reusable zone system so more can be added under future icicle sections. Follow-up fixes **9/20**: reworked from a flat rectangle into a real tapered pool/lens shape (thins to a point at each end, bulges down in the middle) so it actually reads as a pool instead of a band; also fixed a jitter bug where the player could get stuck oscillating in place on the ice (the current's phase was jumping discontinuously every 40px — now a smooth continuous function of position); then the color blobs were found to still be clipped to a plain rectangle instead of the pool's own tapered shape, fixed by clipping to the real pool polygon.
- **Ripple push mechanic — shipped 9/20**: a drip from the icicles above occasionally "lands" in the pool (a few staggered ~2.4-5.2s timers per zone), showing a small expanding ring + splash flash and giving the player a brief small outward push if standing nearby when it lands — ties the icicles and the pool together the way the brainstorm envisioned. Purely time-driven (no persisted state), so the visual and the push always agree on where/when.
- Secret slippy-slidy ice patches near the start — **disabled 9/20** ("it kind of just doesnt make sense"), not deleted; easy to bring back if wanted later
- **General collision default flipped**: front trees no longer block walking past them at ground level — only stop you if you land on top of one. Going forward, ground-level scenery defaults to walkable-past; a real blocking wall/hazard should be an explicit choice, not the default.
- **All three of the owl's tree branches made jumpable — shipped 9/21**: not just the perch — the two smaller decorative branch stubs are now real landing spots too, all sharing one geometry function so the drawn branch and its landing hitbox can never drift apart.
- **Player tilts to match branch slope — shipped 9/21**: standing on an angled branch now visibly tilts the player sprite to match it (same approach as the existing forest rope-bridge tilt), easing back to upright once you leave.
- **A regular front tree repositioned — shipped 9/21**: moved off of the first practice platform to sit in front of the second one instead, per a direct screenshot report of it overlapping the player.
- **Wider gap before the icicle pocket + a new quiet breather stretch after it, with gently falling fractal snowflakes — shipped 9/21**: the flat gap right before the rainbow icicle pocket was widened, and a new open stretch was added just past the icicle pocket/watery ice — deliberately quiet, decorated with a handful of small fractal-branched snowflakes drifting down slowly (visually distinct from the regular ambient dot-snow used everywhere else).
